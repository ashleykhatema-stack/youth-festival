Poster Making Competition

Participation: Each participating College, Institute, or Academic Department may send only 1 student participant. Format: The competition is conducted "On the Spot". The specific theme or subject is revealed by the panel of judges at the time of the event. Paper / Canvas Size: Half Imperial size mount board paper (22 inches in length × 15 inches in breadth), provided on-site by the university. Permitted Mediums: Watercolors, poster colors, or pastel colors. Materials: Participants must bring their own brushes, paints, palettes, and other drawing materials. Time Duration: Exactly 2 hours and 30 minutes.

Evaluation Criteria

Judges assess entries based on the following key aspects: Visualization & Concept: Originality and creative interpretation of the given topic. Delivery of the Subject: Clear visual communication of the theme. Presentation & Composition: Layout, balance, line work, and visual arrangement. Tagline & Impact: Effectiveness of the slogan/tagline and overall aesthetic power.

On the Spot Painting

Participation: Each participating College, Institute, or Academic Department may send only 1 student participant. Format: Conducted "On the Spot". The topic/subject is assigned by the panel of judges right at the start of the competition. Paper / Canvas Size: Half Imperial size drawing paper (22 inches in length × 15 inches in breadth), provided on-site by the university. Permitted Mediums: Watercolors, poster colors, or pastel colors. Materials: Participants must bring their own painting supplies (brushes, paints, palettes, water containers, etc.). Time Duration: Maximum of 2 hours and 30 minutes.

Evaluation Criteria

Judges evaluate the artwork based on six key criteria: Visualization: Creative interpretation of the theme. Delivery of the Subject: Clarity in conveying the assigned message/topc. Composition: Structural balance and visual arrangement. Color Application: Blending, contrast, tone, and technique. Presentation: Neatness and artistic execution. Overall Impact: Final visual appeal and artistic strength.

Collage Making

Participation: Each participating College, Institute, or Academic Department may send only 1 student participant. Format: Conducted "On the Spot". Overall Impact: Artistic expression and visual appeal.

Music (Musika)

Duration: 3 to 7 minutes per entry (varies between solo and ensemble events). Instrumentation: Indigenous/Folk: Must strictly use authentic instruments (e.g., Kulintang, Gangsa, bamboo percussion) without electronic amplification or synthesized backing tracks. Classical/Traditional: Acoustic instruments only (e.g., classical guitar for Harana, standard 14-string set for Rondalla ensembles). Repertoire: Songs or compositions must align with the specified genre (e.g., Kundiman songs must be traditional Tagalog/Visayan art songs, not modern OPM pop ballads). Costume: Attire must reflect the region or historical era associated with the piece (e.g., Barong Tagalog / Baro’t Saya for Rondalla and Kundiman).

Evaluation Criteria

Vocal / Instrumental Technique (30%): Intonation, pitch accuracy, tone quality, breath control, and articulation. Musicality & Expression (25%): Dynamics, tempo interpretation, phrasing, and emotional delivery. Mastery & Precision (20%): Rhythmic accuracy, synchronization (for ensembles like Rondalla), and memorization. Authenticity & Style (15%): Adherence to traditional genre nuances and regional style. Stage Presence & Overall Impact (10%): Poise, confidence, costume appropriateness, and audience engagement

Dance (Sayaw)

Duration: 4 to 8 minutes per routine. Prop & Stage Setup: Folk/Ritual: Props (e.g., bamboo poles for Tinikling, fans/scarves for Singkil) must be authentic to the dance's cultural origin. Setup time is often capped at 1 to 2 minutes. Festival Street Dance: Allowed modular mobile props, provided they fit within stage dimensions. Music: Audio tracks must be high-quality pre-recorded tracks or live traditional musical accompaniments (e.g., live gongs or drums). Attire: Authentic ethnolinguistic attire or festival costumes corresponding accurately to the dance presented.

Evaluation Criteria

Choreography & Composition (30%): Creativity, floor patterns, formation transitions, and thematic structure. Execution & Technique (25%): Coordination, precision, rhythm, body alignment, and footwork mastery. Cultural Authenticity / Concept Fidelity (20%): Accurate movement styles, proper use of traditional props, and authentic attire. Performance & Expression (15%): Energy, facial expressions, showmanship, and unison. Costume & Overall Visual Impact (10%): Visual appeal, color harmony, and stage projection.

Theater & Stage (Dula)

Duration: Ranging from 10 to 15 minutes (for short plays/monologues) up to 30 to 45 minutes (for full one-act plays or Sarsuwela excerpts). Time penalties apply for under/overtime. Cast & Crew: Strict limits on the maximum number of actors on stage and technical crew offstage. Stagecraft & Minimalist Sets: Props and set pieces must be quick to install and strip down (usually within a 3-minute limit). Content: Scripts must be original or appropriately adapted, adhering to ethical standards without vulgarity or uncredited plagiarism.

Evaluation Criteria

Acting & Characterization (35%): Voice projection, diction, emotional conviction, character depth, and actor chemistry. Direction & Staging (25%): Blocking, pacing, use of stage space, and overall dramatic flow. Script & Narrative Structure (20%): Story clarity, thematic strength, dialogue quality, and message effectiveness. Technical & Production Values (10%): Effective integration of sound design, lighting, costumes, and props. Overall Dramatic Impact (10%): Total impression, emotional resonance, and audience connection.

Elocution (Solo Event)

Participation: 1 participant per department/college. Languages Allowed: English, Filipino, or regional languages (e.g., Cebuano/Davao-Binisaya) as designated by the organizers. Topic Selection: Topics are typically announced 24 hours in advance or assigned via a draw at the venue. Time Limit: 4 to 5 minutes. A warning bell sounds at 4 minutes, and a final bell rings at 5 minutes. Points are deducted for under-time or over-time. Format: Delivery must be memorized and delivered orally without reading from notes or prompts

Evaluation Criteria

Content & Substance (30%): Clarity of thought, logical structure, depth of argument, and relevant examples. Oratory & Delivery (30%): Voice modulation, pitch, articulation, pronunciation, and pacing. Language & Diction (20%): Vocabulary choice, grammatical accuracy, and eloquence. Stage Presence & Impact (20%): Confidence, posture, eye contact, non-verbal expression, and audience connection.

Debate (Turncoat / Parliamentary / Standard Debate)

Participation: 1 individual (for Turncoat format) or a 2 to 3 member team (for Standard/Oxford-Oregon format). Format & Preparation: Motion/topic is provided 15 to 30 minutes prior to the round. Turncoat Format: The speaker spends the first half of their time speaking FOR the motion and switches to speaking AGAINST the motion midway upon a signal. Time Limit: 3 to 5 minutes per speaker (plus rebuttal time in team formats). Language: English or Filipino/Tagalog (as specified by the festival guidelines).

Evaluation Criteria

Matter / Argumentation (35%): Strength of logic, evidence, counter-arguments, and refutation of opposing views. Manner / Delivery (30%): Persuasiveness, rhetoric, clarity, voice control, and poise under pressure. Structure & Method (20%): Organization of arguments, adherence to time limits, and effective transitions. Rebuttal / Flexibility (15%): Quality of on-the-spot responses and adaptability (crucial for Turncoat style).

Creative Writing (On-the-Spot Essay / Poetry / Short Story)

Participation: 1 participant per category per institution. Format: Conducted strictly On the Spot. The prompt, theme, or starting line is revealed at the start of the event. Time Limit: 1 hour to 1 hour and 30 minutes. Word Count Limits: Essay Writing: 500 – 800 words. Short Story: 800 – 1,200 words. Poetry: Minimum 16 lines / 4 stanzas (unless free verse is specified). Submission: Written legibly on official provided writing sheets or submitted via a monitored, offline digital platform (if specified).

Evaluation Criteria

Adherence to Theme & Originality (35%): Creative interpretation of the prompt and unique perspective. Structure & Flow (25%): Organization, narrative arc (for stories), or logical development of thoughts (for essays). Command of Language (25%): Vocabulary, stylistic flair, imagery (for poetry), syntax, and grammar. Overall Literary Value & Impact (15%): Emotional resonance, depth of insight, and overall polish.
